# Amazon Interview Question Bank

This page lists 52 questions of the kinds that Amazon is commonly reported to ask: 26 coding, 14 system design, and 12 behavioral. Exact wording varies by interviewer, and Amazon often rewrites a known problem as a story about packages or deliveries. Each coding question links to a lesson with a worked solution.

To practice, set a timer for 30 minutes for a coding question and 35 minutes for a design question. Answer each behavioral question aloud in about two minutes.

## System design

Questions 27 to 37 are for SDE II and above. Questions 38 to 40 are object-oriented design questions, which are more common for SDE I.

27. Design an e-commerce platform like Amazon, with product listings, a shopping cart, and checkout. See [Design Amazon Shopping Cart](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/system-design-interview-crash-course/doc/design-amazon-shopping-cart?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=amazon-interview-guide&utm_content=question-bank).
28. Design an order processing system that handles a sudden rise in orders during a sales event.
29. Design an inventory system across many warehouses.
30. Design Amazon Locker, including the full-locker case and the uncollected-package case.
31. Design an order tracking system.
32. Design a product recommendation service.
33. Design a video streaming service like Prime Video. See [Designing Youtube or Netflix](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/grokking-the-system-design-interview/doc/designing-youtube-or-netflix?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=amazon-interview-guide&utm_content=question-bank).
34. Design a storage service like S3. See [Design Amazon S3](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/system-design-interview-crash-course/doc/design-amazon-s3?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=amazon-interview-guide&utm_content=question-bank).

## Behavioral

Each question tests one Leadership Principle, shown in parentheses. The Leadership Principles are the 16 statements that describe how Amazon expects employees to act.

41. Tell me about a time you changed a plan because of customer feedback. (Customer Obsession)
42. Tell me about a time you took on something outside your area of responsibility. (Ownership)
43. Tell me about a time you made something simpler. (Invent and Simplify)
44. Tell me about a decision you made without all the data you wanted. (Are Right, A Lot)
45. Tell me about something you had to learn quickly. (Learn and Be Curious)
46. Tell me about a time you refused to accept work that was only adequate. (Insist on the Highest Standards)
47. Tell me about a time you acted quickly and accepted a risk. (Bias for Action)
48. Tell me about a time you delivered a result with fewer resources than you wanted. (Frugality)
49. Tell me about a time you found the real cause of a problem in the details. (Dive Deep)
50. Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager. (Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit)
51. Tell me about a time you delivered a project despite a serious obstacle. (Deliver Results)
52. Why do you want to work at Amazon?
